Delete Your BC Khata Account

You can request deletion of your BC Khata account and eligible personal data.

Delete Account from the App

Open BC Khata and follow:

Profile → Account & Security → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app. You may be required to verify your identity again before account deletion.

If You Cannot Access Your Account

You can request account deletion by emailing:

Zentroxstudioscare@gmail.com

Please contact us using your registered mobile number or provide sufficient account details so that we can verify the request.

Data That May Be Deleted

  • Your BC Khata login account
  • Your personal profile information
  • User-specific settings and preferences
  • Eligible personal identifiers linked to your account

Data That May Be Retained

Some committee, contribution, payout, or payment history may be retained in a limited or anonymized form when necessary to preserve the records of other committee participants, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal or security obligations.

Organizer Accounts: If you manage active BC committees, you may need to close or delete those active committees before your organizer account can be deleted.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after identity verification and any necessary account checks.

Contact Us

For questions about account deletion, contact: Zentroxstudioscare@gmail.com
