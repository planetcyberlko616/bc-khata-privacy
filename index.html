Privacy Policy for BC Khata

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

BC Khata is a digital committee and savings record-management application. It helps organizers and members manage BC/committee contributions, payout turns, member records, payment confirmations, and related account information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process information such as:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Firebase authentication identifier
  • Optional profile information
  • Committee and BC details
  • Member contribution and BC share details
  • Payout turn and slot information
  • Payment and collection records
  • Payment confirmation or dispute status
  • Technical information required for app security and operation

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users
  • Connect members with their BC committees
  • Maintain committee and contribution records
  • Calculate payout schedules and BC shares
  • Display member passbooks
  • Allow members to verify organizer-recorded payments
  • Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access
  • Improve app reliability and functionality

3. Committee Data and Visibility

Certain information may be visible to the organizer and relevant members of the same BC committee where required for transparency, record keeping, contribution tracking, and payout management.

4. Payments and Non-Custodial Nature

BC Khata is a record-management application only. BC Khata does not collect, hold, transfer, or manage money on behalf of users. Payments take place directly between committee participants.

BC Khata is not a bank, wallet, payment processor, investment service, or deposit-taking service.

5. Data Storage and Security

We may use services such as Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to provide authentication and store application data.

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be processed by service providers used to operate BC Khata, including Google/Firebase services. Information may also be visible to relevant committee participants as required by the app's functionality.

7. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations.

8. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their BC Khata account and eligible personal data. Some historical committee or payment records may be retained or anonymized where necessary to preserve the integrity of other participants' records or to meet legal, security, or fraud-prevention requirements.

Users may also request account deletion by contacting us at: Zentroxstudioscare@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

BC Khata is not intended for users who cannot legally provide the required consent in their jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be available on this page with the updated effective date.
